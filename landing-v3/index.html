We choose our partners carefully

We don't work with everyone.
Here's how to know if that's you.

Every result on this page came from a partnership where both sides were all-in. We keep it that way by saying no more often than yes.

✓ You're a strong fit if…

  • ▸You're a B2B or enterprise-facing business with a proven offer and real customers.
  • ▸You have a sales team ready to work qualified pipeline.
  • ▸You think in payback and pipeline, and you'll share CRM access so attribution is real.
  • ▸You can commit to at least two quarters. Engines compound; stunts don't.
  • ▸Leadership is at the table, with founder or CXO sponsorship.

✕ We'll pass if…

  • ▸You're pre-revenue or still searching for product-market fit.
  • ▸You want followers, virality, or awards, not pipeline.
  • ▸You need a miracle this month. We won't promise overnight.
  • ▸Marketing is a cost center and every rupee needs three approvals.
  • ▸You won't share data. Without it we'd be guessing, and we don't guess.

Straight answers

Questions serious buyers ask us

If your question isn't here, bring it to the meeting, or email daksh.sharma@iffort.com.

Do you really turn clients away? +
Yes, regularly. Every case study here came from an engagement with real CRM access, a working sales team, and leadership in the room. If the teardown says we're not the right partner, we tell you in week one.
What kind of returns can we actually expect? +
Anyone quoting a return before seeing your data is guessing. Our track record: 5X ROAS and ₹8.25 Cr+ attributed revenue for Cowrks, 45,000+ qualified leads at 40% YoY for SHRM. Phase 1 builds a payback model for your business before you commit further.
Do you guarantee results? +
No, and you should run from anyone who does. What we guarantee is the system: a fixed-scope teardown, sprints that kill losers fast, and spend that scales only behind proven lead quality.
How fast will we see something? +
First signals show inside 30 to 45 days of sprints going live. Real compounding takes two quarters. If you need revenue this month to survive, we're the wrong call and we'll say so.
Why not just hire an in-house marketing team? +
You probably should, eventually. A senior hire takes six-plus months to ramp; we plug in senior operators from day one, then document the playbooks so the engine is yours to run.
Can you work with our existing marketing team? +
That's our favorite setup. We're force multipliers, not replacements: we bring strategy, attribution, and channel expertise; your team brings product depth.
How do you price engagements? +
Phase 1 is fixed-scope and fixed-price. Ongoing engagements are scoped from the teardown and sized to your pipeline math. No percentage-of-ad-spend models.
What actually happens in the first meeting? +
Thirty minutes, no deck, no juniors. You walk us through your funnel; we show you where demand is leaking, what we'd test first, and whether we're the right partner. You leave with clear next steps either way.
