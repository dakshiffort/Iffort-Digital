How an engagement works

No guesswork. A sequence.

We don't start with a retainer. We start by finding exactly where your revenue engine is broken. Then we fix it in phases, each one earning the next.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1-3

The Teardown

A fixed-scope audit of your funnel, attribution, ICPs, and channel economics. We map the gap between where you are and your revenue goal, and we tell you honestly if we can close it.

You get: a written teardown, a payback model, and a go/no-go recommendation. Even if the answer is no.

Phase 2 · Weeks 4-12

Proof Sprints

Hypothesis-driven experiments across the channels the teardown flagged: paid, ABM, AI-first SEO/GEO, content, events. Weekly reviews on CPL, lead quality, and pipeline contribution.

You get: proven channels with unit economics, killed losers, and a CRM-verified pipeline forming.

Phase 3 · Scale

Scale & Systemize

Budgets scale only behind proven ICP alignment, the same algorithmic governance that took SHRM to 45,000+ qualified leads and Cowrks to 5X ROAS. Dashboards report revenue, not activity.

You get: a compounding revenue engine, documented playbooks, and numbers your CFO signs off on.

We choose our partners carefully

We don't work with everyone.
Here's how to know if that's you.

Every result on this page came from a partnership where both sides were all-in. We take on a limited number of new partners so that never changes, which means we say no more often than we say yes.

✓ You're a strong fit if…

  • ▸You're a B2B or enterprise-facing business with a proven offer and real customers. You need demand, not product-market fit.
  • ▸You have a sales team ready to work qualified pipeline. Leads dying in a spreadsheet waste everyone's time.
  • ▸You think in payback and pipeline, not impressions, and you'll give us CRM access so revenue attribution is real.
  • ▸You can commit to at least two quarters. Engines compound; stunts don't.
  • ▸Leadership is at the table. Our best results (SHRM, Cowrks, Mozark) all had founder or CXO sponsorship.

✕ We'll pass if…

  • ▸You're pre-revenue or still searching for product-market fit. We scale engines, we don't find fit.
  • ▸You want followers, virality, or awards. Vanity metrics aren't a deliverable here.
  • ▸You need a miracle this month. We'll show early signals in 30 to 45 days, but we won't promise overnight.
  • ▸Marketing is a cost center in your org and every rupee needs three approvals to test anything.
  • ▸You won't share data. No CRM, no analytics, no attribution means we'd be guessing, and we don't guess.

Straight answers

Questions serious buyers ask us

If your question isn't here, bring it to the meeting, or email daksh.sharma@iffort.com.

Do you really turn clients away? +
Yes, regularly. Every case study on this page came from an engagement where we controlled the variables that matter: real CRM access, a sales team that works the pipeline, and leadership that shows up. When those aren't there, results become luck, and we don't sell luck. If the teardown says we're not the right partner, we'll tell you in week one and point you somewhere better suited. That discipline is exactly why our numbers hold up.
What kind of returns can we actually expect? +
Honest answer: it depends on your deal size, sales cycle, and starting funnel. Anyone who quotes you a return before seeing your data is guessing. What we can show you is our track record: 5X ROAS and ₹8.25 Cr+ in Salesforce-attributed revenue for Cowrks, 45,000+ qualified leads at 40% YoY growth for SHRM, and a predictable SQL pipeline for Channelplay. In Phase 1 we build a payback model specific to your business, so you know the target and how we'll measure it before you commit a rupee to Phase 2.
Do you guarantee results? +
No, and you should run from anyone who does. What we guarantee is the system: a fixed-scope teardown with a go/no-go recommendation, hypothesis-driven sprints where losers get killed fast, and spend that scales only behind proven lead quality. We're selective about who we take on, and that selectivity is the guarantee. We've never needed to hide behind a "results may vary" footnote.
How fast will we see something? +
First signals typically show inside 30 to 45 days of sprints going live: lead flow, CPL benchmarks, message-market resonance. Real compounding takes two quarters. That's when governance kicks in and the engine starts beating its own benchmarks (SHRM's 40% YoY growth came from year-long discipline, not a launch spike). If you need revenue this month to survive, we're the wrong call and we'll say so.
Why not just hire an in-house marketing team? +
You probably should, eventually. A senior marketing leader takes six-plus months to ramp and carries a seven-figure annual cost before they've proven a channel. We plug in as the demand engine now: senior operators across paid, ABM, AI-first SEO/GEO, content, and video from day one. Then we document the playbooks so what we build is yours to run, with your future in-house team if that's the plan.
Can you work with our existing marketing team? +
That's our favorite setup. We're force multipliers, not replacements. We bring the strategy, attribution infrastructure, and channel expertise; your team brings product depth and institutional knowledge. On HP's flagship event we operated as the content fulfillment center inside a much larger stakeholder machine. Egos don't survive long here; pipelines do.
How do you price engagements? +
Phase 1 (the teardown) is fixed-scope and fixed-price, so you know exactly what you're buying. Ongoing engagements are scoped from what the teardown finds, sized to your pipeline math so the fee is a fraction of the revenue target it's accountable for. No percentage-of-ad-spend models: those reward agencies for spending your money, not for making it back.
What actually happens in the first meeting? +
Thirty minutes, no deck, no juniors. You walk us through your funnel and goals; we tell you what we see: where demand is leaking, which channels we'd test first, and whether we're the right partner for it. You leave with a working teardown of your funnel either way. If we're not a fit, you've lost half an hour and gained a second opinion. If we are, you'll know exactly what Phase 1 looks like.
